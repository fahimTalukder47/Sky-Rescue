# FLIGHT MANUAL: SKY RESCUE - CHATTOGRAM CRISIS

**CONFIDENTIAL // PILOT BRIEFING**
**LOCATION:** Chattogram, Bangladesh
**STATUS:** ACTIVE CRISIS

## 🚁 MISSION BRIEFING
Welcome aboard, Pilot. A catastrophic weather anomaly has struck the Chattogram region. Ground extraction is impossible, and thousands of civilians are stranded. You have been selected to pilot our advanced rescue chopper into the heart of the crisis zone. 

Time is short, fuel is limited, and the weather is worsening by the minute. Your mission is to extract survivors from rooftops, manage your payload, and bring them safely to the hospital helipads. We are counting on you. Good luck up there.

## ⚙️ AIRCRAFT CAPABILITIES & SYSTEMS
- **Structural Integrity (3-Strike System):** Your chopper is reinforced. It can take three catastrophic hits before complete failure. Upon impact, emergency deflector shields will engage, giving you a brief window of invulnerability (blinking indicator) to stabilize your flight path.
- **Flight Logs:** Command automatically tracks your career rescues and sector clearances. Your progress is saved in the central database.
- **Cockpit Instrumentation (HUD):** Monitor your vitals. Your dash displays your remaining structural integrity (hearts), fuel reserves, and current passenger payload.
- **Hostile Threats:** Radar has detected an experimental rogue aircraft in Sector 3—codenamed "Rogue Storm Zephyr." You are authorized to use countermeasures if engaged.

## 🗺️ MISSION SECTORS
Your deployment will span three escalating disaster zones:

**Sector 1: Flood Basin**
*   **Conditions:** Submerged cityscapes. Moderate vertical clearance.
*   **Objective:** Familiarize yourself with the flight controls. Extract civilians from standard rooftops and monitor your fuel gauge. Avoid basic debris.

**Sector 2: Mudslide**
*   **Conditions:** Unstable mountainous terrain. Tight quarters.
*   **Objective:** Navigate narrow ravines. Obstacle density is high. You must meet increased extraction quotas to clear this zone.

**Sector 3: Thunderstorm (Red Zone)**
*   **Conditions:** Severe turbulence, zero visibility, towering high-rises (210ft maximum clearance).
*   **Objective:** Survive the supercell. Intel confirms the **Rogue Storm Zephyr** is patrolling this airspace. Evade its plasma tracking weapons and deploy your flares/EMP to neutralize it.

## 🕹️ FLIGHT CONTROLS & S.O.P.

### Flight Yoke (Controls)
*   **Mouse Movement (Cyclic/Collective):** Steer your aircraft. Maintain a steady hand to avoid sudden altitude drops or runaway lifts.
*   **Left Mouse Click:** Interface with dash screens / Deploy countermeasures and flares (when authorized).
*   **Keyboard [SPACEBAR]:** Deploy the rescue hoist to winch survivors aboard.
*   **Keyboard [ESC]:** Suspend flight simulation (Pause) / Return to Command Menu.

### Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)
1.  **Extraction:** Achieve a stable hover directly over stranded civilians and lower your hoist. Do not exceed maximum passenger capacity—overloading will cause a fatal stall.
2.  **Refuel & Evac:** Once at maximum payload, immediately fall back to the designated Hospital Helipad to offload civilians and refuel your tanks.
3.  **Evasion:** Maintain a safe distance from towering structures, migratory bird flocks, and airborne projectiles. You have three strikes per sortie. Keep your bird in the sky.
